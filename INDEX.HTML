THE WEB SHOULD FEEL HUMAN

Not engineered for endless scrolling, but designed for intention, clarity, and connection.

The web has become loud—optimized for engagement instead of understanding. I believe it should return to being a space where people can think, create, and share without being pushed, tracked, or shaped by invisible systems. A human web is one where design is honest, algorithms are transparent, and users understand the structures behind what they see. View source shouldn’t just reveal code—it should reveal values.
